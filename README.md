# ClassKin

### Make invisible students visible.

ClassKin is a school-district technology platform designed to help identify student caregivers, reduce caregiving-related absences, and connect families with childcare assistance they already qualify for.

## The Problem

Some students miss school because they are responsible for caring for younger siblings while a parent works. These students can be treated as truant even though they are filling a childcare gap.

Missed school can snowball into failed classes, lower attendance, and students falling behind.

At the same time, families may qualify for childcare assistance but never receive it because the application process is difficult.

## Our Solution

ClassKin closes the gap between **student attendance, childcare assistance, and family support**.

The process is simple:

1. **Flag** — A counselor identifies a student whose absences may be connected to caregiving.
2. **Screen & File** — ClassKin identifies childcare assistance the family may qualify for and helps organize the application.
3. **Fund the Care** — Eligible assistance helps pay for licensed childcare for the younger sibling.
4. **Back to Class** — The student can return to school instead of staying home to provide care.
5. **Prove It** — The district dashboard tracks attendance recovered and funding preserved.

## Key Features

- Student attendance monitoring
- Caregiving-related absence identification
- Rules-based student risk scoring
- Childcare subsidy workflow
- Care placement tracking
- Attendance and funding projections
- Financial dashboard for districts
- Student-level detail pages
- Role-based Coordinator and Finance views
- Anonymized student identifiers

## Privacy & Trust

ClassKin is designed around a privacy-first approach.

The prototype uses anonymized student codes rather than real student names. The district remains in control of decisions, while families authorize actions involving their information and assistance.

## Prototype
